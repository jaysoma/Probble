Ensemble

Not a prediction — several measured models of the same protein. Each residue is drawn by how far it actually moves between them: steady residues are solid spheres, wandering ones widen into faint probability balls.

The structure glides from each model to the next, so you watch the real conformations morph into one another. The steady core holds still; the loose parts swing through the space they really occupy.

Wobble

Not motion — the distribution. Where AlphaFold is confident, an atom is a sphere. Where it is not, the atom is drawn as the space it could occupy: a ball that widens and thins as confidence falls, so the disordered tails read as the clouds they really are instead of frozen spaghetti. It is the crystallographer's thermal-ellipsoid convention, applied to pLDDT.

The whole structure sways along its own collective modes — the hinge and breathing motions an elastic network model finds for this fold — so domains move against each other the way the physics says they can. The confidence-radius slider exaggerates or suppresses the balls; at zero, every atom is a plain sphere again.
